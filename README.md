## 🇨🇳 中文

把行为心理学、身势学（Kinesics）、近距学（Proxemics）、步态分析学和非言语行为学，翻译成 AI 视频能直接读懂的身体描述，让角色「站怎样、离多远、怎么走、手在干什么」全部可控。四大模块覆盖体态语言（开放/封闭/权力/服从/吸引/回避六组姿态）、空间关系调度（四级距离区 × 朝向组合 × 镜像或断裂行为）、步态系统（身份步态、情绪步态、状态步态）和无意识微动作（自我适应、物体适应、自我梳理、生理性非自愿动作、情绪泄漏）。专门解决三类常见崩坏：两个角色像各拍各的、角色站着不动像机器人、走路像滑行。嵌入时遵循铁律——每镜最多 2–3 个微动作，且只出现在主动作的间隙。

### ✨ 能做什么

- 输入角色关系与情绪状态 → 输出可直接粘贴的身体语言描述
- 六组体态语言：开放、封闭、权力、服从、吸引、回避
- 空间关系调度：四级距离区 × 朝向组合 × 镜像/断裂行为
- 步态系统：身份步态、情绪步态、状态步态，治「走路像滑行」
- 无意识微动作库：自我适应、物体适应、自我梳理、情绪泄漏信号
- 多人场景诊断流程：先判断角色哪里「不对劲」，再做三维定位

## 🇬🇧 English

Translates behavioral psychology, kinesics, proxemics, gait analysis and non-verbal behavior research into body descriptions an AI video model can actually read, so you control how a character stands, how far apart they are, how they walk and what their hands are doing. Four modules cover posture and gesture (six groups: open, closed, power, submission, attraction, avoidance), proxemics staging (four distance zones crossed with orientation, plus mirroring or breaking behavior), the gait system (identity, emotional and state gaits) and unconscious micro-movements (self-adaptors, object adaptors, self-grooming, involuntary physiological actions and emotional leakage). It targets the three classic failures: two characters who look shot separately, a character who stands still like a robot, and walking that looks like sliding. Embedding follows hard rules: at most 2-3 micro-movements per shot, and only in the gaps between primary actions.

### ✨ What it does

- Character relationship plus emotional state in, paste-ready body language description out
- Six posture groups: open, closed, power, submission, attraction and avoidance
- Proxemics staging across four distance zones, orientation combinations and mirror-or-break behavior
- Gait system with identity, emotional and state gaits that fixes sliding-walk animation
- Micro-movement library: self-adaptors, object adaptors, self-grooming and emotional leakage
- Multi-character diagnosis step that first pinpoints what feels off, then sets 3D context
